SEO

I check websites for SEO fundamentals, including keywords, meta tags, headings, indexation, and Google visibility, using tools like Google Search Console and Lighthouse.

How I integrate SEO
I integrate SEO directly into the content by applying the keywords provided during onboarding and placing them naturally throughout the page. I optimize meta titles and descriptions, check H1, H2, and H3 structure, and use Google Search Console to submit XML sitemaps and request indexing for updated pages. I also use Lighthouse to check page performance before publication.
